The Solar Farm Problem

Poor drainage is costing you rows, revenue, and time.

Without proper subsurface drainage, solar developers are forced to space rows farther apart than necessary — and operators spend five days a week managing the consequences.

📐

Forced Wide Spacing

Without drainage, rows must be spaced at 25 ft or more to allow equipment access for maintenance. Every extra foot of spacing is a row you're not generating revenue from.

🔧

Constant Maintenance

Standing water, erosion, and soil saturation around panels creates a year-round maintenance burden. Industry standard is five days a week — every week.

🌧️

Erosion & Panel Damage

Unmanaged runoff between rows undermines panel footings, erodes soil, and accelerates infrastructure degradation — driving up repair costs over time.

Why the Hydro Fix System Works for Solar

Pressure-fed drainage built for solar infrastructure.

No gravel, no fabric, no clogging. Installs with minimal excavation and starts working immediately — without disrupting existing panel infrastructure.

📐

Enables 15 ft row spacing

Proper drainage eliminates the need for wide row clearance, letting you add up to 67% more rows on the same acreage.

💧

Works without slope

Solar farms are often graded flat for panel efficiency. The Hydro Fix System moves water via pressure, not gravity.

♾️

Reduces maintenance to 1 day/month

Once installed, the system eliminates the standing water and erosion that drives daily maintenance needs. 260 days becomes 12.

♻️

Made from 100% recycled plastics

Aligns with the sustainability goals of solar energy projects. Never clogs, never crushes, zero maintenance required.
