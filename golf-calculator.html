The Golf Course Problem

Water is the enemy of consistent play.

Standing water, slow-draining fairways, and waterlogged greens don't just frustrate golfers — they damage turf, erode carts paths, and close revenue-generating holes.

⛳

Waterlogged Fairways

Slow drainage after rain leads to days of cart path restrictions, lost rounds, and turf damage from saturation.

🌿

Green Surface Issues

Poor subsurface drainage causes disease, thatch buildup, and inconsistent roll that frustrates members.

🛣️

Cart Path Erosion

Water concentration along paths and bunkers erodes edges, destabilizes surfaces, and creates safety hazards.

Applications on the Course

Where Hydro Fix makes the difference.

Fairways

Subsurface drainage strips installed along low points eliminate standing water after rain events.

Greens & Surrounds

Perimeter drainage protects green surfaces from chronic waterlogging without disturbing the putting surface.

Bunkers

Bunker floors and faces drain completely after rain, eliminating casual water rulings and turf damage.

Cart Paths

Edge drainage prevents runoff concentration that erodes path edges and destabilizes surfaces.

Tee Boxes

Subsurface drainage keeps tee surfaces firm and consistent, protecting turf from compaction and saturation.

Practice Facilities

Range and practice green drainage ensures your highest-traffic areas stay usable regardless of weather.
