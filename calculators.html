Find out exactly how much your solar farm could earn with smarter drainage.

The Hydro Fix System lets you install rows 40% closer together and cuts maintenance from 260 days a year down to just 12. Enter your details below.

Solar farms are leaving significant revenue on the table — not because of the panels, but because of the spacing required to manage drainage between rows.

Without proper subsurface drainage, standard practice requires 25 ft between array rows and a full-time maintenance worker on site five days a week. With the Hydro Fix System, rows go in at 15 ft apart — and maintenance drops to one worker, one day a month.
