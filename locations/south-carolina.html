Why South Carolina Is Challenging

South Carolina Conditions That Defeat Traditional Drainage.

South Carolina's coastal counties sit at or below sea level with brackish water tables.

🧱

Lowcountry Clay + Tidal Water Table

Coastal SC properties from Charleston to Hilton Head have water tables within 1–3 feet of grade. Drainage systems that depend on water leaving the property by gravity have nowhere for it to go.

🌧️

48–54" Annual Rainfall

Tropical systems compound annual rainfall with surge events.

📐

Local Challenge

Sea-level terrain, brackish groundwater, historic-district excavation restrictions.
