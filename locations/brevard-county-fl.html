Does Hydro Fix serve Brevard County?
Yes — Brevard County is one of our service areas. We evaluate and install throughout 32940, 32955, 32935, 32951 and surrounding Brevard County. Most evaluations can be scheduled within the same week.
Why do drainage systems fail in Brevard County?
Brevard County's barrier island and flatwood soils present extreme drainage challenges. The combination of high water tables, spodic hardpan, and heavy Atlantic storm rainfall creates persistent flooding in residential areas. Traditional gravel and pipe systems lose their void space to soil infiltration within 2–7 years in these conditions. Hydro Fix uses a closed thermoplastic structure with no gravel voids to fill — it performs identically in year 1 and year 25.
How is this different from a French drain?
French drains require slope, gravel, and fabric — all of which fail in Brevard County's soil conditions. Hydro Fix moves water via pressure with no slope requirement, no gravel, and no fabric. It's a fundamentally different mechanism, not just a better version of the same thing.
