Georgia Conditions That Defeat Traditional Drainage.

Georgia Red Clay (Piedmont)

Cherokee, Cobb, Fulton, Forsyth, Gwinnett — all underlain by red Piedmont clay that percolates at 0.1–0.5 inches per hour. Standing water is guaranteed; the only question is how to move it.

🌧️

50–57" Annual Rainfall

Among the highest in the continental US for a non-coastal state.

📐

Local Challenge

Dense residential development on the lowest-percolating soil in the Southeast.
