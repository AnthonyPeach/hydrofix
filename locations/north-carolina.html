Why North Carolina Is Challenging

North Carolina Conditions That Defeat Traditional Drainage.

North Carolina spans three drainage worlds — Piedmont red clay, coastal high water table, and mountain runoff.

🧱

Cecil-Pacolet Clay Loam

NC's Piedmont clay percolates at 0.2–0.6" per hour. That guarantees standing water during any meaningful storm. French drains in this soil fail within 2–7 years as fabric clogs and gravel voids fill.

🌧️

44–48" Annual Rainfall

Increasingly intense individual storm events from a changing climate.

📐

Local Challenge

Explosive Triangle and Charlotte growth on the worst-percolating soil in the state.
