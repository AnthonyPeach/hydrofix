Why Florida Is Challenging

Florida Conditions That Defeat Traditional Drainage.

Florida's high water table, intense convective storms, and sandy-clay subsoils create drainage failures that traditional systems cannot fix.

🧱

Florida Sand + Limestone Shelf

Sandy topsoil over clay or limestone creates a perched water table that saturates yards days after rain. Traditional French drains rely on percolation that simply cannot happen.

🌧️

52–62" Annual Rainfall

Highest in the continental US. Convective storms drop 2–4" in under an hour.

📐

Local Challenge

Sea-level terrain — gravity drainage is mathematically impossible in coastal Florida.
