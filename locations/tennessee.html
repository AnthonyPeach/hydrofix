Why Tennessee Is Challenging

Tennessee Conditions That Defeat Traditional Drainage.

Middle Tennessee's limestone-cherty clay, West Tennessee's loess over Mississippi clay, and East Tennessee's karst valleys each present unique drainage failures.

🧱

Limestone Bedrock + Thin Clay

Middle TN has thin clay over limestone — once you saturate the topsoil, water has nowhere to go. East TN karst creates unpredictable subsurface voids. West TN loess collapses when saturated.

🌧️

48–55" Annual Rainfall

Spring storm cells and remnant tropical systems both deliver intense rainfall.

📐

Local Challenge

Williamson & Rutherford County growth on unforgiving substrate; Memphis floodplain.
