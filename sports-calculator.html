The Sports Complex Problem

Water is the enemy of consistent play.

Standing water, slow-draining infields, and waterlogged outfields don't just frustrate coaches and parents — they cancel revenue, damage turf, and push teams to better-draining facilities.

🏆

Cancelled Tournaments

A single rain event can wipe out an entire weekend of tournament revenue — entry fees, concessions, and facility rentals all gone at once.

⚽

Mud & Standing Water

Waterlogged fields damage turf, create injury liability, and leave a lasting impression on teams deciding where to register next season.

🔧

Endless Maintenance

Regrading, reseeding, and temporary drainage fixes year after year — treating symptoms instead of solving the actual drainage problem.

Why Hydro Fix Works for Sports Fields

Pressure-fed drainage built for high-traffic fields.

No gravel, no fabric, no clogging. Hydro Fix installs with minimal excavation and starts working immediately — without rebuilding your fields.

💧

Works without slope

Doesn't crush under load

⚡
