Why do drainage systems fail so quickly in Smyrna?
Smyrna's dense urban-suburban mix sits on Cobb County Piedmont clay — soil that barely absorbs water even between storms, making recovery time after heavy rain extremely slow. Traditional gravel and pipe systems lose their void space to clay infiltration within 2–7 years. Hydro Fix uses a closed thermoplastic structure with no gravel voids to fill — it performs identically in year 1 and year 25.
How is this different from a French drain?
French drains require slope, gravel, and fabric — all of which fail in Smyrna's clay conditions. Hydro Fix moves water via pressure with no slope requirement, no gravel, and no fabric. It's a fundamentally different mechanism, not just a better version of the same thing.
