Why Brookhaven Homeowners Call Us

The Brookhaven Drainage Problem Is Not Your Fault.

Brookhaven's DeKalb County soils are dense Piedmont clay with naturally low drainage capacity — a problem amplified by the dense tree canopy that channels roof and surface runoff into specific areas repeatedly. Brookhaven's rapid in-town growth and significant renovation activity has exposed many properties to drainage problems that were previously masked by mature tree cover or managed by infrastructure that has since failed.

With individual storms averaging 3.1+ inches, Brookhaven's clay soils are asked to absorb water far faster than they physically can. Every inch of rain generates ~0.6 gallons per square foot of roof surface — thousands of gallons per storm concentrated at your downspouts, foundation, and yard.

The most common drainage problems we see in Brookhaven neighborhoods including Ashford Park, Lynwood Park, Drew Valley, Blackburn Park, and Town Brookhaven:

  • Tree root infiltration destroying existing pipe drainage
  • Basement and crawl space moisture in renovated in-town homes
  • Runoff concentration from neighboring impervious surfaces
  • Limited yard space for traditional drainage solutions

The root cause in almost every Brookhaven case is the same: a gravity-dependent system trying to function in clay soil with no slope. That combination guarantees failure. Our pressure-fed systems don't need slope — they move water via pressure, which works in any terrain and any soil type.

Why do drainage systems fail so quickly in Brookhaven?
Brookhaven's DeKalb County soils are dense Piedmont clay with naturally low drainage capacity — a problem amplified by the dense tree canopy that channels roof and surface runoff into specific areas repeatedly. Traditional gravel and pipe systems lose their void space to clay infiltration within 2–7 years in these conditions. Hydro Fix uses a closed thermoplastic structure with no gravel voids to fill — it performs identically in year 1 and year 25.
How is this different from a French drain?
French drains require slope, gravel, and fabric — all of which fail in Brookhaven's clay conditions. Hydro Fix moves water via pressure with no slope requirement, no gravel, and no fabric. It's a different mechanism entirely, not just a better version of the same thing.
