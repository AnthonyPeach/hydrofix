The Cumming Drainage Problem Is Not Your Fault.

Cumming and Forsyth County sit on Georgia Piedmont clay with percolation rates as low as 0.1–0.3 inches per hour — some of the most challenging drainage conditions in the Metro Atlanta region. Forsyth County's explosive growth over the past decade has brought dense residential development into areas with naturally poor soil drainage, compounding the problem with increased impervious surfaces and overwhelmed community stormwater systems.

Why do drainage systems fail so quickly in Cumming?
Cumming and Forsyth County sit on Georgia Piedmont clay with percolation rates as low as 0.1–0.3 inches per hour — some of the most challenging drainage conditions in the Metro Atlanta region. Traditional gravel and pipe systems lose their void space to clay infiltration within 2–7 years. Hydro Fix uses a closed thermoplastic structure with no gravel voids to fill — it performs identically in year 1 and year 25.
How is this different from a French drain?
French drains require slope, gravel, and fabric — all of which fail in Cumming's clay conditions. Hydro Fix moves water via pressure with no slope requirement, no gravel, and no fabric. It's a fundamentally different mechanism, not just a better version of the same thing.
