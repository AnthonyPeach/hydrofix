💧 What It Is

Water moves through it — not around it.

Traditional French drains rely on slope and a bed of gravel wrapped in fabric. Over time they silt up, the fabric clogs, and the pipe can crush — and the water comes back.

HydroBlox is different. Made from 100% recycled thermoplastics, it wicks and channels water through the material itself using capillary pressure — no slope required, no gravel, no fabric, nothing to clog. It's rated for 40,000 lbs of crush force and carries a 25+ year life expectancy.

What we solve with it

  • Standing water & soggy yards
  • Foundation & basement moisture
  • Failed or clogged French drains
  • Driveway, patio & hardscape drainage
  • Downspout & surface runoff

Finishing a basement? Drainage first is the smart order of operations — solve the water, then build.
