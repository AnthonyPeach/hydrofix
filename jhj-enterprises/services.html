Common Questions

Good to know.

Do you charge for an estimate?
No. Every estimate is complimentary and on-site — we come out to you, look at the project, and give you an honest quote with no obligation.
Is there a job too small (or too big)?
No job is too small or too big for our knowledgeable crews. We've remodeled homes from roof to basement, and we're just as happy to handle a single room or repair.
What is HydroBlox, and why not just a French drain?
HydroBlox is a permanent, pressure-fed drainage system made from 100% recycled thermoplastics. Unlike a French drain, it needs no slope, gravel or fabric, it's rated for 40,000 lbs of crush force, and it never clogs — so it keeps working for 25+ years with zero maintenance.
