Why Drainage Fails

Flexible perforated pipe collapses under soil pressure and root intrusion.

Common Questions

Georgia Homeowners Ask These Questions Before Booking.


Is Hydro Fix right for every yard?
Not always — and we'll tell you that upfront. Some yards have soil percolation rates too low for any system to work without additional steps. That's exactly why we start with an evaluation, not an installation.
How is this different from a French drain?
French drains rely on gravel and perforated pipe that clog over time and require slope to function. Hydro Fix moves water using pressure — no slope required, no gravel, no fabric, nothing to clog. It's a fundamentally different mechanism.
How long does installation take?
Most residential projects are completed in a single day. Larger stormwater systems may take two days. We give you a clear timeline before any work begins.
Do I need to maintain it afterward?
Nothing. Hydro Fix has no moving parts, no fabric to replace, and no gravel to flush. Once installed, it's done.
What if my property isn't a good fit?
We'll tell you honestly, explain why, and suggest what might work better. Our goal is to solve your water problem — not sell you something you don't need.
